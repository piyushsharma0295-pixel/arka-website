How We Work

Our Proven Process

A structured, transparent approach that keeps you informed and in control at every stage of your project.

01

Initial Consultation

Free consultation to understand your vision, requirements, site conditions, and project goals. We listen first, then plan.

02

Site Visit & Assessment

On-site assessment of your plot, existing structure, or proposed location to gather accurate data for design.

03

Concept Design

Initial design concepts with floor plans, 3D visualizations, and layout options for your review and feedback.

04

Structural Analysis

Complete structural analysis using STAAD Pro / ETABS to ensure your building is engineered for safety and longevity.

05

BOQ & Cost Estimation

Detailed Bill of Quantities and cost estimation so you know exactly what your project will cost before breaking ground.

06

Construction Drawings

Complete working drawings, approval drawings, and shop drawings ready for construction and municipal approvals.

07

Execution Support

Regular site visits and technical guidance during construction to ensure quality control and design compliance.

08

Project Completion

Final inspection, completion certificate, as-built drawings, and handover documentation — your project, delivered perfectly.
